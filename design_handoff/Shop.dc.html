2新項目：一捧土 · 紀念 Memorial — 依文化本地化

Two design notes before the screens. (1) Timezone is not culture. A Taipei-based user on a work trip to Berlin would get a 墓碑, and a Taiwanese-American in Los Angeles would get one permanently. So the timezone only suggests a form on first death; the real switch is a setting, and the shipped default for any locale we can't map confidently is the neutral 盆栽 — see 2f. (2) The memorial screens are the only ones in the app with no neon. Ash, earth and bone; the countdown's green/amber/red stops at the moment of the explosion. Pet death is also the app's first real punishment mechanic — worth confirming it doesn't undercut the anti-guilt positioning. My proposal: 復活 cannot be bought with 秒幣 (three consecutive anchors instead); what the shop sells is the memorial's appearance.
