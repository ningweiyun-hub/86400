86,400

After Hours

Every local calendar day you receive 86,400 seconds; they cannot be paused, saved, or carried into tomorrow. Daytime is calm and still. As the day burns down the interface heats up, brightens, grows and finally shakes.

Pressure is the core mechanic — a deliberate reversal of PRD §12's "never manufacture anxiety". Because of that, §13.4's counter-metrics (seconds-display opt-out rate, notification opt-out rate, reported time anxiety) are the guardrails, and Settings carries a single Night intensity switch that disables the whole escalation.

01 Pressure Everything is a function of p

03 Themes Four skins · one engine

Anxiety Neon is the default, not the only, skin. Number size runs 92 + p×22 px — a target, not a guarantee. Glyph count drops in steps (six characters at 19,800, five at 7,800) while the size grows smoothly, so on a narrow phone the two peak together in the early evening and the number outgrows the hero. Because the hero is overflow:hidden that reads as a silently truncated digit, not a broken layout, so the number is measured against the hero width and clamped down when it will not fit: ~93px at 20:30 on a 390pt phone where a 430pt phone still shows 106px. Fit wins over size, and the late-day 110–114px is untouched wherever there is room. Stages 2 and 3 are gated on p, not on elapsed time — p > 0.55 lands at ≈19:40, not at the 13:12 an elapsed reading would imply. Only stage 4 reads the raw elapsed percentage, and it additionally requires Night intensity to be on, so the switch removes the shake and the stage-4 sentence together. prefers-reduced-motion drops stages 2–4 to a static state, freezes the spark, and removes the scanline layer entirely.
05 The fuse Not a progress bar
Bomb20px circle inside a 22px box at the left end. radial-gradient(circle at 34% 32%, bg L+0.1, bg L−0.04 75%) — derived from the live ramp, not a fixed hex — with a 1px accent rim, an inset bottom shadow and a 10×4px rope stub on its right edge. The bomb is only the default head: an equipped companion replaces it with a 26px glyph on the same anchor, carrying the same pulse and rattle.
RopeUnburnt: repeating-linear-gradient(115deg, paper/.16→.26 0 3px, paper/.34→.54 3px 6px), width = remaining percent of the day, 5px tall — 4px on Field Ops, which also hatches the track behind it and adds 1px rope edges.
TailBurnt-out remainder on the right is 3px of linear-gradient(to left, ink/.04, ink/.13) — a gradient, not a flat fill — and it rides the skin's ink token, so a light skin darkens where a dark skin lightens.
FlameSits at the burn point (left: remaining%) and burns right → left: outer flare 22→48px, white-cored spark 9→17px with a 10→28px glow, three embers drifting up-right on n-ember loops. Stage 4 adds +16 / +6 / +12 px on top of those three.
Motionn-bombpulse 1.4s (scale 1 → 1.12) past p > 0.75; n-rattle .22s (±2.5px) at stage 4, in sync with the number. With Night intensity off, p is pinned to 0 and the whole flame goes inert: flare, spark and all three embers stop, and the embers drop to zero opacity. Freezing the animation but leaving the spark lit is the half-measure the switch forbids.

09 Rules that hold Carried from the PRD
No pause, no borrow, no carry-over

The countdown recomputes from the wall clock on every tick and on resume — never a background counter. Accuracy after resume ≤1s. Rolls to a new day at local midnight without relaunch. Works offline.

Unnamed time, never "wasted"

Untracked time is unnamed time: "Not wasted — just not recorded today." Rest, play and doing nothing can all be rated very worth it.

No streaks, no score

No penalties, no comparison with other users, no productivity score. Ratings are the user's own and never derived from the activity name.

One escalation switch

Night intensity pins p to 0 and disables stages 2–4 entirely. The three §13.4 opt-out metrics are the guardrails on whether the mechanic is earning its keep.

Full zh / en parity

The ZH / EN switch swaps every string — block names, categories, receipt rows and past-receipt entries included.

Preview hooks do not ship

There is no DEMO row in the build. Review runs on query parameters with no UI entry: ?preview=HH:MM pins the clock, ?theme=<id> previews a skin without buying it, and ?memorial=1 opens the midnight settle. None of the three write to localStorage.

10 Platform tokens Shared layout · native controls

Do not ship a hex palette. The colours are a function of the clock, so both platforms need the interpolator itself: an OKLCH→sRGB conversion and the three-stop table per skin. Everything downstream — theme objects, glows, scanline opacity — is derived once per minute and pushed into the platform's colour scheme.

Five levers, none of them motion
ScaleThe number is the tile. 52–56px on a 170px cell, up from 38–44 in the calm state.
HeatAccent, border and the outer glow all ride the same p. At 0.95 the tile edge is accent, not hairline.
PositionThe flame sits almost on the bomb. Nothing has to move for that to read.
CompanionOne line: something is still waiting on the fuse. This is the lever the shake used to carry.
Deadline"Settles at midnight" — a stated end, not an implied one.
Refresh budget
iOS WidgetKitTimelineProvider, ~1 entry / 15 min
Live ActivityText(timerInterval:) — per second, free
Android GlanceWorkManager, 15 min floor
In-app1 Hz from the wall clock

A widget that cannot tick must print its refresh time and round to the minute — never show a stale per-second figure as if it were live. The fuse advances at the same granularity as the number it sits under.

Decided Widgets never animate. Stage 4's shake is in-app only, and there is no widget-side shake setting to expose — the setting is gone, not defaulted off. A widget that shakes all evening is an uninstall, and neither WidgetKit nor Glance animates cheaply. Everything above stops at stage 2's vocabulary: colour, glow and scale.
Privacy Anchor text is the only personal string on a lock screen. When Widget privacy is on it collapses to Set · text hidden — the cell keeps its size so the layout does not shift when the setting changes. The companion cell is never hidden: it carries no personal content. Every tap lands directly on the matching action.

Reduce MotionStages 2–4 collapse to stage 1: colour and glow still escalate, nothing moves. The spark freezes at the burn point.

16MemorialsShipped · differs from the regional concept

When the fuse burns out at midnight the companion does not disappear — the day settles, the companion is blown off the fuse, and what is left is a handful of earth that becomes a memorial object. The flow branches from the countdown screen, not from Shop, because that is where the fuse was. The build resolves two things the concept left open: the form is chosen, not routed, and a memorial can be revived.

ToneA memorial is a record, not a penalty. No score changes, nothing is deducted, no streak breaks — the same rules as an unfinished Anchor. The vocabulary is funerary, not punitive: nothing in this flow uses the words fail, lose or waste. The toasts are still the product's voice, though — "{name} is back. Do not blow it again." is pressure, not comfort.
The settle · four beats at local midnight
Beat 1 · 23:59:59

The fuse reaches the head. The number holds at stage 4 and the running block auto-ends at 23:59:59.

Beat 2 · the flash

A red bloom — mem-flash .55s over radial-gradient(rgba(255,77,94,.55), transparent 62%). It plays inside the 168px memorial stage, not across the whole screen.

Beat 3 · the companion goes

Embers fall on mem-ember .9s at +0.15s while the companion scales up and out on mem-pet-blow .7s.

Beat 4 · the earth rises

A handful of earth lifts in on mem-earth-rise 1s at +0.75s and holds. The user then chooses to enshrine it or skip.

The whole sequence runs about 1.75s inside a 168×160 stage. Under prefers-reduced-motion the flash, embers and companion are set to display:none and the earth is shown at its final state immediately — the beat is skipped, not slowed. The sequence plays once per fallen companion and is tracked in memory (ui.memBoomDone), so a refresh does not replay it.

Three objects, not five. There is no ancestral tablet and no memorial-hall object: the hall is a screen that lists memorials you already hold, and there is no sharing anywhere in the product, so the concept's opt-in share link has nothing to attach to. The card frame, the data and the rite mechanic are identical across forms — only the art box, the free skin and the two ritual verbs change.
Locale suggests · Settings decides
suggestMemorialForm()      // runs once, only if untouched
  /^zh/                  → 'urn'
  /^(en|de|fr|es|pt|it)/ → 'headstone'
  otherwise              → 'pot'

S.memorialForm        // pot | urn | headstone | none
S.memorialFormTouched // locks the suggestion out

// changing the form affects the NEXT enshrinement only;
// memorials already placed keep the form they were given.
Deliberately weaker than routing. The device locale supplies one suggestion, once, and only if the user has never touched the setting; after that Settings owns it and the suggestion never fires again. Nothing infers faith, and re-rendering existing memorials into a new object — which the concept called for — does not happen: placed memorials are immutable.
Revival · death is a loop
REVIVE_NEEDED = 3          // consecutive Anchor days

memorial.status
  'pending'  // fallen, not yet enshrined
  'placed'   // enshrined
  'skipped'  // user declined the memorial
  'revived'  // back on the fuse

revive = { progress, needed, lastAnchorDay, fallenId }
// same-day undo: revivedFrom / revivedOn / revivedNeed
The concept listed this as an open question — "is death a loop or an ending?" It is a loop. Three consecutive days with the Anchor finished puts the companion back on the fuse; the progress pips render as .mem-revive i. Reviving is reversible within the same day, which is why the pre-revival status is stored rather than overwritten.
The rite · a daily visit with a streak
WhenA local reminder at RITE_AT = '20:00', offered on top of the three daily notifications rather than inside that budget.
StreakCounted only if rite.lastDay is today or yesterday. A missed visit does not zero the stored streak — it stops the UI from showing a stale one.
GrowthPot only, 0–7, rendered as .mem-leaves i.on. It is the one memorial that keeps changing, which is what the concept wanted from the plant.
VerbsTwo per form, from ritesForForm(). No rite is described as sacred and none is required.
Memorial skins · a second purchasable axis

18 skins across the three forms, 70–140 coins, one free per form (pot_clay · urn_plain · stone_plain). A skin swaps the hatch hue and the border on the art box and nothing else — the form determines the silhouette. That makes three independent purchase tracks in the product: themes (skins for the ramp), companions (who sits on the fuse) and memorial skins (what holds them afterwards). The design system covers the first; the other two are specified in the Shop handoff.

Rules the flow must not break
Never infer faith

The locale picks an object, once, as a suggestion. It never picks a belief, and no rite is described as sacred or required.

Neutral default

The pot is the fallback for every unlisted locale — the one object with no funerary or religious specificity.

Opt out completely

none is a first-class form. The companion still falls; nothing is enshrined and no memorial screen appears.

Nothing is shared

There is no share link, no hall others can enter, no comparison. The hall lists only your own memorials.

Language ≠ form

ZH / EN is a separate switch. A user can read English and keep the urn.

The settle is not a score

No coins are deducted, no streak breaks, no rating changes. The loss is the companion, and that is the whole penalty.
